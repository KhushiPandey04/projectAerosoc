RESEARCH POINT 2: RATE OF INTEREST (ROI) +

Save bits where nothing is happening

🛰️
Over 60% of satellite imagery is empty terrain.

Half of satellite captures are clouds, oceans, and desert sand. The ROI compressor applies aggressive loss where blur does not matter, saving maximum bitrate budget for cities, airports, and shipping harbors.
